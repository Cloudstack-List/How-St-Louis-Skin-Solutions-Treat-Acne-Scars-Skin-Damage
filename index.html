Understanding Acne Scars and Skin Damage

Acne scars develop when inflamed blemishes affect deeper layers of skin. During healing, the body produces collagen to repair damaged tissue. When collagen forms unevenly, visible scars may appear.

Common types include:

  • Ice pick scars – narrow and deep marks in the skin
  • Boxcar scars – wider depressions with defined edges
  • Rolling scars – uneven skin texture caused by tissue bands
  • Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation – dark spots left after breakouts

Skin damage may also result from several factors:

  • Sun exposure
  • Aging and natural collagen loss
  • Environmental stressors
  • Previous skin inflammation

Common Treatments Used for Skin Rejuvenation

Laser-Based Skin Treatments

Laser technologies are frequently used to support skin renewal. These treatments work by directing controlled energy into targeted layers of skin.

Potential benefits may include:

  • smoother skin texture
  • improved skin tone
  • reduced appearance of scars

Laser procedures are commonly used for both cosmetic rejuvenation and certain medical skin concerns.

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) Therapy

Platelet-rich plasma therapy uses a small sample of a patient’s own blood to create a concentrated solution of platelets. Platelets contain growth factors that may support the body’s natural repair processes.

PRP is often used in aesthetic medicine to support:

  • skin rejuvenation
  • tissue repair
  • hair restoration

Because the treatment uses the patient’s own biological material, it is widely considered a regenerative technique.

Photobiomodulation (Light Therapy)

Photobiomodulation therapy uses specific wavelengths of light to support cellular activity in the skin. This approach is non-invasive and is often used as part of wellness or recovery treatments.

Potential benefits may include:

  • improved circulation
  • support for skin recovery
  • reduced inflammation

Light-based therapies are becoming more common in modern aesthetic and wellness clinics.

Red Light Therapy

Red light therapy is another light-based treatment that has gained attention in recent years. It uses red and near-infrared light to support skin health and cellular activity.

Some individuals seek red light therapy to support:

  • skin appearance
  • circulation
  • tissue recovery

Many clinics now combine light therapies with other aesthetic treatments for comprehensive skin care.
